Fetch settled REST bars into `fixtures/local/live/rest/` (sequential, paced)

Usage: qp live bars [OPTIONS] --symbols <SYMBOLS> --period <PERIOD> --start <START>

Options:
      --symbols <SYMBOLS>  Comma-separated symbols or `@file`
      --period <PERIOD>    REST period (`1min`, `5min`, `1hour`, `1day`, `15min`, …)
      --start <START>      First local date `YYYY-MM-DD`
      --end <END>          Last local date (default: `start`)
      --unadjusted         Unadjusted prices (`divide=false`)
  -h, --help               Print help
